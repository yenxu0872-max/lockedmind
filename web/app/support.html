Lockedmind for iPhone

Support

Lockedmind is private AI that runs entirely on your iPhone. Here's what it needs, and answers to common questions.

What you need: an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer), iOS 26 or later, and Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri.

Common questions

It says this iPhone can't run Lockedmind

Lockedmind uses Apple's on-device model, which only runs on iPhones that support Apple Intelligence: iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max and every iPhone since.

It says "Getting ready…"

Your iPhone is still downloading Apple's on-device model after Apple Intelligence was turned on. Keep it on Wi-Fi and charging. Lockedmind moves on by itself when the download is done.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Lockedmind never connects to the internet, so it works the same in Airplane Mode. That also means it can't browse the web or know today's news.

It wouldn't answer a question

Apple's on-device model has a built-in safety filter, and it sometimes declines harmless questions too. Try putting it another way.

It says my message is too long

The on-device model can only read a few pages of text at once. Send a shorter message, or split it into parts. In long chats, Lockedmind keeps the most recent part of the conversation in mind.

Where are my chats, and how do I move them to a new iPhone?

Only on your iPhone. They're left out of backups unless you turn on Settings › Include chats in backups; with that on, restoring a backup to a new iPhone brings them along.

Can it read photos, scans and PDFs?

Yes. Tap the + button to attach a photo, scan a paper document with the camera, or pick a PDF or text file. Lockedmind reads the words in them on your iPhone and answers questions about them; on iOS 27 it can also look at photos. Very long documents are shortened to fit what the model can read at once.

Dictation says it isn't available

Lockedmind only uses speech recognition that runs on your iPhone. If your iPhone can't recognise your language on the device, Lockedmind won't send your voice anywhere instead. The keyboard's own dictation button still works.

What does "Online · nothing sent" mean?

It shows whether your iPhone is connected to the internet. Lockedmind never uses the connection: online or offline, every answer is made on your iPhone and nothing you write is sent.

How do I lock Lockedmind?

Turn on Settings › Lock with Face ID. Lockedmind then hides your chats whenever you leave it and opens again only with your Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. It needs a passcode set on your iPhone.

Can it get things wrong?

Yes. Like any AI it can make mistakes, so check anything important.

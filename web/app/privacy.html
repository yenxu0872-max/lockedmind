Lockedmind

Lockedmind for iPhone

Privacy Policy

The short version: Lockedmind collects nothing. What you write stays on your iPhone.

We don't collect, receive, store, share or sell any information about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

How Lockedmind works

Answers are written on your iPhone by Apple's on-device language model, which is part of Apple Intelligence. Lockedmind uses only that on-device model, never a server.

The app contains no networking code. It doesn't send your messages, your chats or anything else to us, to Apple or to anyone. It works the same in Airplane Mode.

What is kept on your iPhone

  • Your chats, in the app's private storage, protected by iOS data protection.
  • Your settings: answer length, and, only if you type them, your first name and a short note about yourself. These are given to the model with your chats, on your iPhone, so it can answer you better.

By default your chats are left out of iCloud and computer backups, so they exist only on your iPhone. If you turn on Settings › Include chats in backups, they become part of your iPhone's backups, which are handled by Apple or by your computer, not by us.

The Face ID lock

If you turn on Settings › Lock with Face ID, iOS checks your Face ID, Touch ID or passcode whenever you open Lockedmind. Lockedmind never sees your face, fingerprint or passcode; iOS only tells it whether unlocking worked.

Deleting your data

Swipe left on a chat to delete it, or use Settings › Delete all chats. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Apple Intelligence

Apple Intelligence is part of iOS, made by Apple. Turning it on and downloading its models is between you and Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. Lockedmind only asks the on-device model for answers.

Children

Lockedmind collects no information from anyone, including children.
